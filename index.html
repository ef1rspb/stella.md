GNSS-independent navigation

Fixed-wing drones that keep flying when GNSS fails

A compact module that keeps fixed-wing aircraft on course when satellite navigation is jammed or spoofed.

What we build

A compact add-on module for ArduPilot fixed-wing aircraft. A downward camera and an onboard computer recognise the terrain below, match it against map imagery and send absolute position fixes to the autopilot — without any satellite or radio signal. Between fixes, calibrated dead reckoning keeps the aircraft on course.

  • Two independent matchers must agree before a fix is used — designed for zero false positions.
  • Works with the ArduPilot autopilot operators already use.
  • Works with any orthophoto or satellite imagery.
  • Cold start and launch without GNSS or radio link.
